Our Approach

Why Milne

Remodels aren't predictable. Neither are repairs. We assume drawings aren't the whole story and build our process around early investigation, clear documentation, and steady communication.

Our job is to make uncertainty manageable.

Identifying problems early

We investigate before we price. What's behind the wall matters as much as what's in front of it.

Communicating scope changes clearly

When conditions change, you hear about it immediately. With documentation, not excuses.

Protecting schedule and margin

Surprises don't have to derail projects. Preparation and communication keep things moving.

Passing inspection without drama

We know what inspectors look for. Our work holds up to scrutiny because we build it that way.
