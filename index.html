Featured Project

Robert Libke Police Station Monument

A monument honoring the original school, built entirely with original brick and precast. This project required precision craftsmanship to preserve the historical integrity while creating something that will stand for generations.

Every brick was carefully selected and placed to match the character of the original structure. The precast elements were integrated seamlessly, creating a cohesive tribute to the building's history.

The result: a lasting monument that honors the past while serving the community's future.
